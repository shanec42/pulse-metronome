# Pulse Metronome

A free, open-source **pacing aid for meditation and relaxation** for Android. It plays a gentle metronome that starts near your pulse and slowly leads it slower.

**Pulse Metronome is not a medical device.** It does not diagnose, treat or monitor any condition.
