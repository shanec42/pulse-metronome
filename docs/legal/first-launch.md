# Before you begin

Pulse Metronome is a **pacing aid for meditation and relaxation.**

- **It is not a medical device.** It does not diagnose, treat or monitor any condition. Don't use it to make medical decisions. If you have a heart condition, talk to your doctor before using it.
- **Readings come from your oximeter, with no guarantee.** The app shows what the device sends and can't confirm it's accurate.
- **The heart trace is an illustration**, not a recording of your heart rhythm.
- **Your data stays on your phone.** Nothing is sent anywhere. Android's device backup may include your session history.
