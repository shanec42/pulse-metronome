# Pulse Metronome — How to use

I've created this app to help people achieve a pulse focused meditation. This works best if you are sitting or lying in a comfortable relaxed position, with the pulse oximeter on a finger. Once it starts, listen for the tic of the metronome, and ideally feel it align with your pulse. It doesn't matter where you feel your pulse, anywhere it's evident will work. At the beginning of a session, the tic is designed to land directly on your pulse. As it starts declining, it may sound like it's landing slightly after, or slightly before your pulse. That is normal. As both the BPM and your pulse decline, they will generally fall in and out of sync with each other, until your target is reached.

If you are someone who can't typically relax and feel your pulse, you're not excluded from using this app. Sitting quietly, listening to the tic of the metronome, and just imagining that the tic is your pulse will typically suffice.

You don't need a pulse oximeter to use this app. You can achieve a pulse focused meditation without the live pulse sync. Before I built this app, I used to use a standard metronome set to a fixed rate. It doesn't work quite as well, but it does work.
